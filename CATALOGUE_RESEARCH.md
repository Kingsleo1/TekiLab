# Catalogue research and pricing provenance

Research dates: 24–25 November 2026.

The seed at public/catalogue/test-catalogue.json contains 1,086 laboratory tests/panels and 240 imaging entries. It is a broad starter catalogue, not every possible test or protocol worldwide. Authorized staff can add unlisted services and validate their local templates, availability and fees.

## Laboratory sources

- SYNLAB Nigeria: https://www.synlab.com.ng/tests/ and its public WooCommerce product catalogue. Retrieved 1,003 products; excluded ten gift cards. No marketing descriptions or images were imported.
- K&H published walk-in pricing: https://khlabs.lovable.app/pricing . Lab-to-lab fees were not mixed into walk-in ranges.
- NIMR CHVG March 2025 specialist handbill: https://nimr.gov.ng/nimr/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/CHVG-Client-Information-Handbill-March-2025.pdf . Its historical effective date is retained.
- Scope reference: https://loinc.org/kb/users-guide/introduction/scope-of-loinc and https://loinc.org/kb/users-guide/evolving-principles-for-naming-collections/collections-as-orders-and-observations . No LOINC codes were invented.

Laboratory entries with published observations: 1,021. Matching is limited to normalized exact names and explicit aliases. A displayed range represents the captured provider observations, which can differ by method, location and date; it is not a national market minimum/maximum. Single-source fees are labelled as published references.

Existing facility templates/fees and ten rapid presets were retained separately. Imported names do not generate result measurements, reference intervals, clinical findings or turnaround promises.

## Imaging sources

- Memfys Hospital radiology pricing: https://memfys.net/patients/pricing/ .
- St Nicholas Hospital services: https://saintnicholashospital.com/services/ .
- RadiologyInfo (ACR/RSNA) modality/procedure families: https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/test-treatment .
- General modality scope: https://medlineplus.gov/diagnosticimaging.html .

The 240 imaging entries include common MRI, MRA, CT/angiography, X-ray, ultrasound, Doppler, mammography, fluoroscopy, nuclear medicine/PET, DEXA, dental, cardiac ultrasound and image-guided procedure families. Availability at TekiLab is not inferred from these references.

Fifty-four entries carry published Memfys price observations, 45 retain existing starter facility fees, and 141 require a quote. Equipment strength and contrast variants are separate when the source distinguishes them. Contrast, consultation, expedited reporting and other extras are not silently added or assumed included.

NHIA reimbursement tariffs were not substituted for private walk-in retail prices.

## Local management and billing

Every price observation retains its provider, URL, retrieval date and historical date where supplied. Staff-set facility fees are distinguished from reference prices; source observations remain available for review. Unknown fees remain unquoted and do not create a free test.

Changes are branch-scoped, revision-checked and audited. Orders retain their agreed fee snapshot. Archive stops new ordering without deleting history. Source names and custom catalogue codes are not claims of clinical accreditation, universal coding equivalence, or onsite equipment capability.

Before real service delivery, the facility must review fees, protocols, availability, referral arrangements and clinical SOPs. Actual payments require the approved payment-provider setup; actual Astra migration and public hosting remain on hold.
